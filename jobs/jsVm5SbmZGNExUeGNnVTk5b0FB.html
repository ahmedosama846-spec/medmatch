Requirements

  • •Qualifications • Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) degree from an accredited medical university.
  • •Valid medical license issued by the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS).
  • •Minimum of five years of clinical experience with at least two years in occupational health.
  • •Proven experience in emergency medicine and trauma care in industrial settings.
  • •Valid driving license with the ability to travel to remote project sites.

Responsibilities

  • •Occupational Health Surveillance This position conducts pre-employment and periodic medical examinations to ensure employee fitness for duty in hazardous environments.
  • •Clinic Operations Management The Site Doctor manages the daily operations of the site clinic including inventory control of pharmaceuticals and medical equipment.
  • •Health Risk Assessment The role identifies and assesses health risks associated with operational processes and recommends preventive measures to management.
  • •Health Data Reporting This position prepares and submits monthly health statistics and reports to the HSE Manager and regulatory authorities as required.
