Join the Paediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) at King Faisal Specialist Hospital & Research Centre (KFSH&RC), Jeddah, working within a highly specialised paediatric critical-care environment. The PICU provides care for critically ill patients from newborn to 14 years of age, with continuous monitoring and advanced intervention. The unit provides specialist experience including: • Care of critically ill infants and children • Continuous intensive monitoring and intervention • Post-cardiac surgery care for children following congenital heart disease repair • Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation
