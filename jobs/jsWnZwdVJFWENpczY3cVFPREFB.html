Consultant– General Surgery (Part-Time)

Requirements

  • •Participate in ongoing training and adopt system enhancements and best practices, collaborating with relevant support teams to optimise performance and user experience.
  • •Skills • 3 years minimum in general surgery clinical practice • Medical degree (MBBS, MD, or equivalent) from an accredited medical school.
  • •Board certification in General Surgery.
  • •Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS) classification and registration as General Surgery Consultant.
  • •Proven experience as a General Surgery Consultant or similar role, preferably in a hospital or outpatient clinical setting.

Responsibilities

  • •Job Description Provides specialized general surgery care within the ICC physical clinic on a part-time, in-person basis, addressing a broad range of surgical conditions.
