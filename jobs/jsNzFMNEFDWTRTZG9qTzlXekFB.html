Staff Nurse - PICU

Dallah Health · Other
Nurse Full-time Salary not stated 0–5 yrs

The staff nurse is accountable to the PICU Charge Nurse for the delivery of high quality care of the patients at Dallah Hospital. The post holder is responsible for ensuring attainment of agreed clinical standards and professional development supported by colleagues and the Director of Nursing. During the time period for which she is responsible, assesses the overall needs of the individual patients, assists in preparation and implements the nursing care process as indicated to ensure optimal patient care.
