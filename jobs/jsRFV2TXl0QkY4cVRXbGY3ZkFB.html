Dental Hygienist: Elevate Patient Care & Prevention

National Medical Care Co. · Other
Dentist Full-time Salary not stated 0–5 yrs

National Medical Care Co. seeks a Dental Hygienist to conduct patient screenings, perform cleanings, and advise on oral health and prevention.
