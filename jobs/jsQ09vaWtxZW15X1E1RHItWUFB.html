Director - Medical Operation Center (SA1)

Qiddiya Investment Company · Other
Consultant Full-time Salary not stated 5–10 yrs

Director Medical Operation Center job purpose s to direct the daily medical operations of Qiddiya City on behalf of Medical Operations, ensuring that contracted medical services are delivered safely, continuously and to the required standard across all city assets. The Director serves as the principal operational interface between Medical Operations, the contracted medical provider, the City Command Unit (CCU) and asset operators. The role provides operational and clinical oversight of the provider's performance, leads medical readiness for assets and events, and directs medical emergency prep

Requirements

  • •For physicians: Board certification in Emergency Medicine (Saudi Board, or an equivalent recognized by the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties).
  • •Preferred: fellowship or subspecialty training in EMS, disaster medicine or prehospital care.
  • •Non-physician: classified by SCFHS as a senior level in their profession.
  • •Current advanced life support certifications in adult, pediatric and trauma care.
  • •Direct experience in EMS or prehospital care in a medical direction, oversight or operational leadership role.

Responsibilities

  • •The Director serves as the principal operational interface between Medical Operations, the contracted medical provider, the City Command Unit (CCU) and asset operators.
  • •The role provides operational and clinical oversight of the provider's performance, leads medical readiness for assets and events, and directs medical emergency preparedness and incident response.
  • •Requirements Responsibilities: • Run the daily medical operation of Qiddiya City and make sure every asset has medical cover at or above the Medical Minimum Operating Requirements.
  • •Act as the day-to-day operational counterpart to the contracted medical provider and verify that contracted staffing, equipment and standards are delivered.
