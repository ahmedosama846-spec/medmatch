Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026 · Applies to MedMatch (ahmedosama846-spec.github.io/medmatch)

The short version: your CV and profile live in your account, readable only by you. We never sell data, never show your CV to employers, and never run ads or tracking cookies. You can delete everything at any time from your Dashboard.

Who we are

MedMatch is an independent healthcare job-matching service covering Saudi Arabia. Job listings are aggregated from public sources and employer portals; each listing belongs to its original source. Data protection contact: ahmedosama846@gmail.com. This policy is written to meet the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone
  • Your CV is never sent to employers. When you click Apply, you leave MedMatch and deal with the employer directly on their own site — what you share with them is between you and them
  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, no third-party ad pixels
  • No passwords are stored anywhere — sign-in uses one-time email links

Third-party processors

  • Supabase — account authentication and the encrypted database holding your profile (data processed on our instructions, protected by row-level security)
  • Brevo — delivers the weekly job-match email. It processes your email address and the job titles in your digest. Turn alerts off anytime: Dashboard → Preferences
  • CDNs (jsDelivr, cdnjs) — deliver code libraries (PDF reader, AI model). They see a file request from your IP, nothing else

Your rights (PDPL)

  • Access & correct — your full profile is visible and editable via Dashboard → Upload CV / Preferences
  • Delete — Dashboard → Privacy & your data → "Delete my data & sign out". This erases your CV, profile, saved jobs, preferences and activity immediately
  • Withdraw consent — turning off email alerts or deleting your data takes effect at once
  • Complain — you may contact us at the address above, or the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA)

Note: deleting your data removes everything in your account row. A minimal sign-in record (email address only) may remain in the authentication log for security purposes; email us if you want that removed too.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your data, it is gone from the live database immediately. Automated backups may retain it for up to 7 days before permanent erasure.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Your data row is isolated by row-level security — no other user, and no one with the public site key, can read it. Sign-in uses magic links, so there is no password database to breach.

Job listings disclaimer

Listings are aggregated from public job boards and employer portals and belong to their original sources. We check apply links weekly, but always verify details — salary, requirements, legitimacy — with the employer before sharing documents or paying anything. No genuine employer charges candidates fees.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and signed-in users are notified on their next visit. Questions: ahmedosama846@gmail.com.
